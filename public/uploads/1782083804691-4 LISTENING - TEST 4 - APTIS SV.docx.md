Question 17: Listen to an advertising expert talking about the advertising industry. 

* What does the expert say about the negative side of advertising?

A. Series are damaged by overexposure.   
B. Advertisements might sometimes be repetitive, which is annoying.   
C. Advertising costs the same amount of money to produce a movie.   
Đáp án đúng: Series are damaged by overexposure

* In what way can advertising affect sports?

A. They help to attract more fans.   
B.. They can boost ticket sales and sales of sports-related items.   
C They can generate negative publicity for the sport   
Đáp án đúng: They can generate negative publicity for the sport   
Giải thích :  
 Advertising in sports has become a double-edged sword. Many fans grow tired of seeing the same commercials repeatedly during games and events. This constant bombardment can lead to frustration, causing viewers to feel disconnected from the sport they love. When ads overshadow the actual games, it detracts from the excitement and intensity that fans crave, making them less enthusiastic about tuning in. **Therefore, many TV series have been damaged by overexposure. **  
In terms of Sports, **overexposure can generate negative publicity for the sport itself**. Fans may start to associate their favorite teams and players with annoying advertisements rather than the thrilling action on the field. This shift in perception can affect attendance at games and viewership on TV, as people become disillusioned with the sport's focus on commercial interest. As a result, teams and leagues must carefully consider how much advertising they allow, ensuring it doesn't take away from the game experience.

In the end, while advertising can bring in revenue and promote the sport, it's crucial to find a balance. Too much exposure can lead to viewer fatigue and diminish the overall appeal of the series. By prioritizing the game itself and minimizing intrusive ads, sports organizations can maintain fan engagement and protect their brand image.
